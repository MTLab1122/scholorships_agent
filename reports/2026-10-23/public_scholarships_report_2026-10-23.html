Methodology & Source Verification Framework

This intelligence report is compiled automatically every day at 07:00 Pakistan Standard Time (PKT, UTC+5). Every listing is verified strictly against official domains:

  • Tier 1 (Official): Direct government domains (*.gov.pk, hec.gov.pk, ef.kp.gov.pk, scholarships.kpitb.online), university domains (*.edu.pk), and accountable sponsor platforms. Marked with a green Confirmed badge.
  • Tier 2 (Official Secondary): Partner republishing notices (e.g. university SFAO notice on federal schemes). Marked Via Partner.
  • Tier 3 (Unofficial): Aggregators and press announcements. Used for discovery only; retained as Unverified until confirmed on official domains.

Important Disclaimer: While automated verification checks apply links, deadlines, and verbatim eligibility requirements daily, students are strongly advised to consult the respective university Students Financial Aid Office (SFAO) or sponsor portal to confirm exact document submission and attestation procedures before applying.
